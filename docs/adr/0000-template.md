title: ADR Template
---

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

## Context

Describe the situation, constraints, forces, and problem that require a decision.

Include links to issues, docs, or prior ADRs when they matter.

## Decision

State the decision clearly.

Use direct language: "We will..." or "The project uses...".

## Rationale

Explain why this option was chosen.

Focus on tradeoffs, not just benefits.

## Consequences

Describe what becomes easier, harder, constrained, or required because of this decision.

Include follow-up work when needed.

## Alternatives Considered

- Option A — why it was not chosen.
- Option B — why it was not chosen.

## Review Trigger

Describe what future evidence should cause the team to revisit this decision.
